# Version 1.0.0

Released 29 October 2026.

Numerical reproduction package for the nine analyses listed in `README.md`: material
relations (Figure 4), associations (Figure 5), microstructure (Figure 6), temporal
windows (Figure 7), the observed frontier (Figure 8), the saved five-property model
evaluations (Figure 9), the 40,401-cell target plane (Figure 10), the addition maps
(Figures 11 and 12) and the source-equal dose analysis (Figure S23). The package also
contains the extraction benchmark materials (Table S1) and the source-reviewed tensile
series (Figure S24).
