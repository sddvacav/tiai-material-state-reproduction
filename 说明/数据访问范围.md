# Data access

This release contains the numerical inputs, Source Data and recorded results of the
nine analyses listed in `README.md`, together with the analysis code.
`数据/复现输入索引.json` lists every numerical input with its SHA-256, and
`数据/发布文件清单.json` lists every file of the restored analysis directory.

The selection data contain 1,111 frozen paired candidates from 336 sources, their
feature matrices and predictions, and source-level results. Endpoint qualification
removes 41 pairs; the within-source comparison uses the 913 retained candidates from
the 168 sources that offer at least two alternatives.

The addition data contain 3,347 source-context endpoint intervals and 603 aligned
strength–elongation intervals (Figures 11 and 12) and the source-equal protocol with
3,646 condition series, 11,128 dose-level points and 536 summary cells (Figure S23).
The complete 40,401-cell target plane of Figure 10 is included with its cohort
definition.

Every record keeps its source DOI and evidence locator. Publisher PDFs and XML,
original source figures, the complete curated corpus and private research records
are not distributed; a DOI identifies a publication but does not grant permission to
redistribute it. Requests for further research data may be addressed to the
corresponding author, subject to source-licensing restrictions.

The authors' curation and numerical results are covered by `说明/数据许可.md`; the
software is covered by `LICENSE`.
